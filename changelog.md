# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- Replaced the temperature widget with a depth/height gauge: the barometric
  pressure sensor is now used to show meters (or feet) below the water
  surface (depth) or above it (height), with a matching history chart,
  shallowest/deepest markers, and a new "Calibrate to Surface" menu action.
- Added a "Salt water" setting to account for the higher density of salt
  water when converting pressure to depth.
- Dropped support for devices without a barometric altimeter.
